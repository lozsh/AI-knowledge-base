    深度学习参考文献

    更新于 2026-11-03

    本篇是《深度学习》教材（即本知识库 01–06 章笔记的原始教材）参考文献章的整理版。原文为 PDF 抄录，含页眉页码残留与少量格式损坏；本页已按主题重新分组、去重并修复明显损坏的条目。条目格式为 [作者, 年份] 中文译名（英文原题）. 出处.，出处一律使用通行缩写。完整列表见原资料，本页收录原章节全部 150 余条中经整理后的版本。

    教材、综述与数学基础

    • [Bishop, 2006] 模式识别与机器学习（Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning）. Springer.
    • [Goodfellow et al., 2016] 深度学习（Deep Learning）. MIT Press. http://www.deeplearningbook.org
    • [Russell & Norvig, 2016] 人工智能：一种现代方法（Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach）. Pearson.
    • [Strang, 1993] 线性代数导论（Introduction to Linear Algebra）. Wellesley-Cambridge Press.
    • [Bollobas, 1999] 线性分析（Linear Analysis）. Cambridge University Press.
    • [Boyd & Vandenberghe, 2004] 凸优化（Convex Optimization）. Cambridge University Press.
    • [Nesterov, 2018] 凸优化讲义（Lectures on Convex Optimization）. Springer.
    • [Wasserman, 2013] 统计学大全：统计推断简明课程（All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference）. Springer.
    • [Petersen et al., 2008] 矩阵手册（The Matrix Cookbook）. 丹麦技术大学.
    • [Kolter, 2008] 线性代数复习与参考（Linear Algebra Review and Reference）. 斯坦福在线讲义（原文链接损坏，此处信息不完整）.
    • [Hennessy & Patterson, 2011] 计算机体系结构：量化方法（Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach）. Elsevier.
    • [Lipton & Steinhardt, 2018] 机器学习学术中的令人担忧的趋势（Troubling Trends in Machine Learning Scholarship）. arXiv:1807.03341.

    优化、初始化与训练技巧

    • [Polyak, 1964] 一些加速迭代方法收敛的方法（Some Methods of Speeding up the Convergence of Iteration Methods）. USSR Computational Mathematics and Mathematical Physics, 4(5).
    • [Nesterov & Vial, 2000] 随机规划的置信水平解（Confidence Level Solutions for Stochastic Programming）. Stochastic Programming E-Print Series.
    • [Duchi et al., 2011] 用于在线学习和随机优化的自适应次梯度方法（Adaptive Subgradient Methods for Online Learning and Stochastic Optimization，即 AdaGrad）. JMLR, 12.
    • [Kingma & Ba, 2014] Adam：一种随机优化方法（Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization）. arXiv:1412.6980.
    • [Zeiler, 2012] AdaDelta：一种自适应学习率方法（AdaDelta: An Adaptive Learning Rate Method）. arXiv:1212.5701.
    • [Tieleman & Hinton, 2012] Lecture 6.5 — RMSProp：将梯度除以近期幅度的移动平均值（RMSProp: Divide the Gradient by a Running Average of Its Recent Magnitude）. COURSERA: Neural Networks for Machine Learning, 4(2).
    • [Sutskever et al., 2013] 论初始化和动量在深度学习中的重要性（On the Importance of Initialization and Momentum in Deep Learning）. ICML.
    • [Goh, 2017] 动量为什么真正有效（Why Momentum Really Works）. Distill, doi:10.23915/distill.00006.
    • [Flammarion & Bach, 2015] 从平均到加速，只差一个步长（From Averaging to Acceleration, There Is Only a Step-Size）. COLT.
    • [Reddi et al., 2019] 论 Adam 及更远处算法的收敛性（On the Convergence of Adam and Beyond）. arXiv:1904.09237.
    • [Zaheer et al., 2018] 非凸优化的自适应方法（Adaptive Methods for Nonconvex Optimization）. NeurIPS.
    • [Loshchilov & Hutter, 2016] SGDR：带热重启的随机梯度下降（SGDR: Stochastic Gradient Descent with Warm Restarts）. arXiv:1608.03983.
    • [Gotmare et al., 2018] 深入审视深度学习启发式方法：学习率重启、预热和蒸馏（A Closer Look at Deep Learning Heuristics: Learning Rate Restarts, Warmup and Distillation）. arXiv:1810.13243.
    • [Izmailov et al., 2018] 权重平均导致更宽的极优解和更好的泛化（Averaging Weights Leads to Wider Optima and Better Generalization，即 SWA）. arXiv:1803.05407.
    • [Glorot & Bengio, 2010] 理解训练深度前馈神经网络的困难（Understanding the Difficulty of Training Deep Feedforward Neural Networks）. AISTATS.
    • [He et al., 2015] 深入整流器：在 ImageNet 分类上超越人类水平的表现（Delving Deep into Rectifiers: Surpassing Human-Level Performance on ImageNet Classification，即 PReLU 初始化）. ICCV.
    • [Srivastava et al., 2014] Dropout：一种防止神经网络过拟合的简单方法（Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting）. JMLR, 15(1).
    • [Bishop, 1995] 噪声训练等价于吉洪诺夫正则化（Training with Noise Is Equivalent to Tikhonov Regularization）. Neural Computation, 7(1).
    • [You et al., 2017] 卷积网络的大批量训练（Large Batch Training of Convolutional Networks）. arXiv:1708.03888.

    归一化

    • [Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015] 批量归一化：通过减少内部协变量偏移加速深度网络训练（Batch Normalization: Accelerating Deep Network Training by Reducing Internal Covariate Shift）. arXiv:1502.03167.
    • [Ioffe, 2017] 批量重归一化：减少批量归一化模型中的小批量依赖（Batch Renormalization: Towards Reducing Minibatch Dependence in Batch-Normalized Models）. NeurIPS.
    • [Ba et al., 2016] 层归一化（Layer Normalization）. arXiv:1607.06450.
    • [Santurkar et al., 2018] 批量归一化如何帮助优化？（How Does Batch Normalization Help Optimization?）. NeurIPS.
    • [Luo et al., 2018] 理解批量归一化中的正则化（Towards Understanding Regularization in Batch Normalization）. arXiv 预印本.
    • [Teye et al., 2018] 批量归一化深度网络的贝叶斯不确定性估计（Bayesian Uncertainty Estimation for Batch Normalized Deep Networks）. arXiv:1802.06455.

    卷积神经网络与计算机视觉

    • [LeCun et al., 1998] 应用于文档识别的基于梯度的学习（Gradient-Based Learning Applied to Document Recognition，即 LeNet）. Proceedings of the IEEE, 86(11).
    • [Krizhevsky et al., 2012] 使用深度卷积神经网络的 ImageNet 分类（ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks，即 AlexNet）. NeurIPS.
    • [Simonyan & Zisserman, 2014] 用于大规模图像识别的极深卷积网络（Very Deep Convolutional Networks for Large-Scale Image Recognition，即 VGG）. arXiv:1409.1556.
    • [He et al., 2016a] 用于图像识别的深度残差学习（Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition，即 ResNet）. CVPR.
    • [He et al., 2016b] 深度残差网络中的恒等映射（Identity Mappings in Deep Residual Networks）. ECCV.
    • [Huang et al., 2017] 密集连接卷积网络（Densely Connected Convolutional Networks，即 DenseNet）. CVPR.
    • [Szegedy et al., 2015] 深入卷积（Going Deeper with Convolutions，即 GoogLeNet/Inception v1）. CVPR.
    • [Szegedy et al., 2016] 重新思考计算机视觉的 Inception 架构（Rethinking the Inception Architecture for Computer Vision）. CVPR.
    • [Szegedy et al., 2017] Inception-v4、Inception-ResNet 以及残差连接对学习的影响（Inception-v4, Inception-ResNet and the Impact of Residual Connections on Learning）. AAAI.
    • [Lin et al., 2013] 网中网（Network in Network，即 NiN）. arXiv:1312.4400.
    • [Hu et al., 2018] 压缩与激励网络（Squeeze-and-Excitation Networks，即 SENet）. CVPR.
    • [Zhang et al., 2021] 超越全连接层：用四元数参数化超复数乘法，仅需 1/n 参数（Beyond Fully-Connected Layers with Quaternions: Parameterization of Hypercomplex Multiplications with 1/n Parameters）. ICLR.
    • [Dosovitskiy et al., 2021] 一张图片等于 16×16 个词：用于大规模图像识别的 Transformer（An Image Is Worth 16x16 Words: Transformers for Image Recognition at Scale，即 ViT）. ICLR.
    • [Dumoulin & Visin, 2016] 深度学习卷积算术指南（A Guide to Convolution Arithmetic for Deep Learning）. arXiv:1603.07285.
    • [Lin et al., 2010] ImageNet 分类：快速描述符编码和大规模 SVM 训练（ImageNet Classification: Fast Descriptor Coding and Large-Scale SVM Training）. 大规模视觉识别挑战赛.
    • [Xiao et al., 2018] 动力学等距和 CNN 的均值场理论：如何训练 10,000 层普通卷积神经网络（Dynamical Isometry and a Mean Field Theory of CNNs: How to Train 10,000-Layer Vanilla Convolutional Neural Networks）. ICML.

    目标检测与分割

    • [Girshick et al., 2014] 用于精确目标检测和语义分割的丰富特征层次结构（Rich Feature Hierarchies for Accurate Object Detection and Semantic Segmentation，即 R-CNN）. CVPR.
    • [Girshick, 2015] Fast R-CNN. ICCV.
    • [Ren et al., 2015] Faster R-CNN：通过区域提议网络实现实时目标检测（Faster R-CNN: Towards Real-Time Object Detection with Region Proposal Networks）. NeurIPS.
    • [He et al., 2017] Mask R-CNN. ICCV.
    • [Uijlings et al., 2013] 用于目标识别的选择性搜索（Selective Search for Object Recognition）. IJCV, 104(2).
    • [Redmon et al., 2016] You Only Look Once：统一的实时目标检测（You Only Look Once: Unified, Real-Time Object Detection，即 YOLO）. CVPR.
